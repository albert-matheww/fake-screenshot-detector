## Single-source and disagreement checks

- Line 98 (and line 88): the claim about what "published screenshot work" does and finds rests on venkatesh2026screenshot alone (abstract-level). Reworded above to say "the one screenshot-specific pipeline found". That source describes screenshots as having "heavy recompression" and evaluates "with real JPEG ELA", which differs from the lossless-PNG premise of this report's first-version audit (line 17, 59 of 60 PNG). The report already declines to compare numbers (line 88); consider adding one clause saying the premises differ.
- Calibration (lines 85, 222): zhao2026docforge's calibration is F1-oriented on labelled domain images (including tampered pixels), while this project calibrates on genuine images at a fixed false-alarm rate. The report presents this as agreement; it is support for "in-domain threshold adaptation" only.
- ronfleux2026docqt vs this report: the source's benefit depends on models that ingest quantisation tables; this report's detector does not, so the source cannot be used as a direct justification for the design choice (lines 85 and 295 as originally worded).
- No pair of sources was found that contradicts each other on a claim the report relies on (dhouib2026doctamper, yu2025fsts, xu2026reality and zhao2026docforge agree that synthetic or fixed-threshold results transfer poorly).
- STFD dataset card (hf_STFD.txt) carries two licence statements: YAML tag `cc-by-4.0` and the text "released for academic research purposes only". The report states neither licence, so no change is needed; avoid quoting the tag alone if a licence is ever stated.
